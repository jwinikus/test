About

Helping engineering students build confidence, curiosity, and capability.

Dr. Jennifer Winikus is a Teaching Associate Professor whose work centers on engineering education, innovative curriculum design, and student success. Her teaching philosophy combines rigorous technical instruction with practical mentorship and inclusive learning experiences.

She is deeply involved in initiatives that strengthen academic quality, support professional development, and connect undergraduate learning to the real-world challenges facing the engineering profession.
